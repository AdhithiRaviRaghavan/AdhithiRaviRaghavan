# Hi, I'm Adhithi <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MartinHeinz/MartinHeinz/master/wave.gif" width="28px">

Computational biologist (PhD) working across genomics, microbial biology and multi-omics. I build reproducible R and Python pipelines for high-throughput sequencing data and work closely with experimental scientists, from study design to interpretation.

## What I work on
- **NGS analysis & pipelines**: end-to-end workflows from sequencing QC and alignment to variant calling, annotation and prioritisation, across short- and long-read DNA and RNA sequencing and chromatin profiling
- **Genome biology**: DNA double-strand break regulation and repair, genome stability, repetitive and telomeric regions, chromatin, and engineered genomes (Synthetic Yeast Genome Project, Sc2.0)
- **Microbial genomics**: 16S and shotgun metagenomics, diversity analysis, comparative genomics, enzyme and pathway annotation
- **Protein engineering & optogenetics**: design of light-controlled gene expression systems and split enzymes in bacteria
- **Genome engineering**: analysis of CRISPR knockout, CRISPRa/i and CAST experiments in bacteria and yeast, including optogenetically controlled guides
- **Protein & sequence analysis**: mining proteomes for metabolic enzymes with sequence-based methods, AlphaFold and IUPred
- **Statistics & data science**: experimental design, batch and confounder control, mixed-effects and Bayesian models, GWAS and population genetics, multiple-testing correction, unsupervised ML (PCA, UMAP, clustering)

## Experience
- **Princeton University**: Postdoctoral Research Associate, Bioengineering. Genome engineering and metabolic pathway analysis in bacteria and yeast
- **New York University**: PhD, Biology. NGS pipelines, DNA break regulation, Synthetic Yeast Genome Project (Sc2.0)
- **University of British Columbia**: MSc, Chemical & Biological Engineering. Optogenetics, protein engineering and design, metagenomics
- **Northeastern University**: MS, Pharmaceutical Sciences. Drug formulation and HPLC analysis
